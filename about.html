About the team

We're a team that runs on grit and grease.

BaconBots was founded by a small group of students who wanted a place to build, break, and rebuild things — together. Today we're a full FIRST Tech Challenge team competing every season and recruiting the next generation of engineers.

Our story

How we got here.

  • 2023

    Founded in Portola Valley

    A handful of students and two very patient mentors decided to found a robotics team. We spent our first year getting the program off it's feet and learning how to participate in FIRST - we didn't actually compete in our rookie year.

  • 2024

    First competition season

    We rolled into our first FTC qualifier as rookies with a robot that didn't really work — and walked away hungry for more.

  • Today

    Year two, leveling up

    Eight members, two years of competing under our belt, and a lot of ideas for the next build season. In our second season, we finally began holding our own as a team. We almost made it to playoffs in our second competition, and almost won against an alliance by ourselves.
