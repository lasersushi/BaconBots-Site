About the team

We're a team that runs on grit and grease.

BaconBots was founded by a small group of students who wanted a place to build, break, and rebuild things — together. As one of the few middle school teams in our competition bracket, we start each season at a slight disadvantage — but one we've learned to overcome. Today we're a full FIRST Tech Challenge team competing every season and recruiting the next generation of engineers. We also serve as a feeder team for Team 751, Barn2Robotics — our upper school's FRC program.

Our story

How we got here.

  • 2023

    Founded in Portola Valley

    A handful of students and two very patient mentors decided to start a robotics team. We spent our first year getting the program off the ground and learning the ropes of FIRST — we didn't actually compete in our rookie year.

  • 2024

    First competition season

    We rolled into our first FTC qualifier as rookies with a robot that barely worked — and walked away hungry for more.

  • Today

    Year two, leveling up

    Nine members, two seasons under our belt, and a stack of ideas for the next build. In our second year we finally started holding our own — coming close to playoffs at our second qualifier, and nearly taking down a full alliance with just our robot on the field.

Our sister team

Team 751, Barn2Robotics.

Barn2Robotics is the upper school FIRST Robotics Competition (FRC) team at Woodside Priory. As a feeder program for Team 751, BaconBots members carry their skills — CAD, software, wiring, driving, and strategy — straight into FRC when they move up to high school. Both teams share a mission: getting more students hands-on with real engineering, season after season.

Every one of our alumni has gone on to continue their FIRST journey with Barn2Robotics. FTC students grow into FRC leaders, and that continuity is a big part of why the program works.

FAQ

How do I join the team?

We recruit new members each fall at the start of the school year. Feel free to come over to the tinkerlab to learn more. You can also email us at the address above and we'll add you to the interest list and let you know about info sessions.

Can my company sponsor BaconBots?

Yes, please! We are always looking for new sponsors. Head over to our sponsors page for tier details, then email us and we'll send the full sponsorship packet within a couple of days.

Are you officially affiliated with FIRST?

BaconBots is a registered FIRST Tech Challenge team.

Where is the lab located?

We primarily practice out of C3, the Tinkerlab.

What is the commitment?

BaconBots is a flexible program. We work with athletics and the theater to coordinate schedules. We typically meet during lunch and after school a few times a week. Schedules are determined at the start of every school year, and we are flexible with the level of commitment you want to bring to the team.

Want to join us?

We recruit new members each fall. New mentors and sponsors are welcome year-round.
