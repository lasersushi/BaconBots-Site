About the team

We're a team that runs on grit and grease.

BaconBots was founded by a small group of students who wanted a place to build, break, and rebuild things — together. Today we're a full FIRST Tech Challenge team competing every season, mentoring younger teams, and recruiting the next generation of engineers.

Our story

How we got here.

From a couple of kids in a garage to a full competing team — the short version.

  • 2023

    Founded in Portola Valley

    A handful of students and one very patient parent decided a robotics team sounded better than another semester of nothing to build.

  • 2024

    First competition season

    We rolled into our first FTC qualifier as rookies with a robot that mostly worked — and walked away hungry for more.

  • Today

    Year two, leveling up

    Eight members, two years of competing under our belt, and a lot of ideas for the next build season.
