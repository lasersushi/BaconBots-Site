Why sponsor BaconBots

Your support actually moves the needle.

  • Direct student impact.  Funds buy parts, tools, and travel — never overhead.
  • Visible in your community.  Logos appear at competitions, on the robot, and on team apparel seen by hundreds of families.
  • Tax-deductible.  We work through a registered nonprofit fiscal sponsor.
  • Pipeline to future engineers.  Our alumni go on to top engineering programs — many return as mentors.
  • STEM outreach multiplier.  Every dollar reaches dozens more students through our FLL mentorship program.
