Sponsorship tiers

Pick the level that fits.

All tiers include a tax-deductible receipt through our nonprofit fiscal sponsor.

Silver — $250+

Logo on our website and competition pit banner. Shout-outs at outreach events.

  • Web logo placement
  • Pit banner inclusion
  • Annual thank-you letter from the team

Gold — $1,000+

Everything in Silver, plus prominent branding and an invitation to a shop tour.

  • Logo on team t-shirts
  • Logo on robot
  • Private shop tour & demo

Platinum — $5,000+

Top-tier branding, a custom student-built demo at your event, and a season recap presentation.

  • Premier logo placement everywhere
  • On-site student demo at your event
  • End-of-season recap presentation
  • First right of refusal for next season
