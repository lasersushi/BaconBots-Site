Robots & projects

Every season, a new bot.

Each year FTC reveals a new game, and we get a few months to design, build, and program a robot from scratch. Here are the ones we've sent into the arena.

Decode

Season 2025–26 · DECODE

This is our current robot for the Decode season. This has been our most successful robot to date! Our robot this year used a fixed angle shooter and a mecanum drivetrain. It used drive encoders and april tag localization. We averaged 68.4 points solo. This was our first robot to ever have full advanced autonomous capabilities, thanks to an advanced pathing algorithm called Pedro Pathing.

  • ScoringFixed-angle shooter
  • AutoPedro Pathing

How we build

Kickoff to competition.

Our season runs in four overlapping phases — strategy, design, build, and iterate.

1. Strategy

We watch the kickoff stream, score every task, and decide what kind of robot will win this game.

2. Design

Mechanisms get sketched, prototyped in cardboard and PVC, then locked into CAD before parts get cut.

3. Build

Parallel sub-teams build mechanisms, wiring, and code — meeting weekly to integrate.

4. Iterate

Drive practice, scrim matches, and post-mortems keep us improving all the way to championship.
