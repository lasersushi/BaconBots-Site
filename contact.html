FAQ

Quick answers.

How do I join the team?

We recruit new members each fall. Email us at the address above and we'll add you to the interest list and let you know about info nights.

Can my company sponsor BaconBots?

Yes, please! Head over to our sponsors page for tier details, then email us and we'll send the full sponsorship packet within a couple of days.

Will you do a demo at our school or event?

Probably! We love demos. Send us details (date, audience size, location) and we'll see what we can put together.

Do you mentor younger teams?

Yes — we run a free FLL mentorship program for local middle schools. Get in touch and we'll connect you with the program lead.

Are you officially affiliated with FIRST?

BaconBots is a registered FIRST Tech Challenge team. The FIRST® trademarks belong to FIRST and we use them under their brand guidelines.
